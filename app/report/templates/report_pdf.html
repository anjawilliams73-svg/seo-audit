{# ── 6. HOW WE CHECK ── #}

6 — How We Check

What does "On-site" cover?
Everything on your own website — page titles, descriptions, headings, images, speed, and code quality. These are things you (or your developer) can fix directly.
What does "Indexing" mean?
Whether Google can find and read your pages. We check your sitemap, robots.txt, broken pages, redirects, and duplicate content.
What is "Linking & Social"?
Other websites linking to yours and your social media presence. This takes time and outreach to improve.
Where does backlink data come from?
We use Common Crawl, a free public index of the web. It gives a partial picture — for a full backlink report, tools like Ahrefs or Semrush are needed.
What is Domain Authority?
A score that predicts how well your site ranks. It requires paid tools (Moz, Ahrefs) so we cannot include it here.
What is AI Readiness?
How well your content is structured for AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews — Schema markup, FAQ sections, and clear question-and-answer formats.
What is Structured Data (Schema)?
Hidden code that tells Google what your page is about. Good Schema can earn you star ratings or FAQ answers shown directly in Google search results.
