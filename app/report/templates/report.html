How we check your site

Here's what this report actually looks at and where the data comes from.

What does "On-site" cover?
Everything on your own website — page titles, descriptions, headings, images, page speed, and the code quality. These are things you (or your developer) can fix directly.
What does "Indexing" mean?
Whether Google can find and read your pages. We check your sitemap, robots.txt file, broken pages, redirects, and duplicate content.
What is "Linking & Social"?
Other websites linking to yours (backlinks) and your social media presence. This is the hardest area to improve — it takes time and outreach.
Where does the backlink data come from?
We use Common Crawl, a free public database of the web. It gives a partial picture — for a full backlink report, tools like Ahrefs or Semrush are needed.
What is Domain Authority?
A score (invented by Moz) that predicts how well your site ranks. It's not available through free tools, so we can't show it here.
What is AI Readiness?
How well your content is structured for AI tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews. We check for Schema markup, FAQ sections, and clear question-and-answer formats.
What is Structured Data (Schema)?
Hidden code that tells Google what your page is about — a product, a review, an FAQ, a local business, etc. Good Schema can get you "rich results" in Google (star ratings, FAQs shown directly in search).
