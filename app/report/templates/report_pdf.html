Off-Page Action Plan

1. Get listed in local & niche directories
Submit to Google Business Profile, Yelp, industry associations, local chambers of commerce. Easy, free backlinks that also help local SEO.
2. Write guest posts for sites in your niche
Reach out to blogs in your industry and offer to write an article with a link back to your site.
3. Get mentioned in local press
Sponsor a local event, issue a press release, or reach out to local journalists. Local newspaper sites link to businesses they cover.
4. Ask suppliers and partners to link to you
Ask suppliers or partner businesses to add your site to their "stockists" or "partners" page.
5. Create content worth linking to
Guides, data, original research, and useful tools attract natural links over time.

6 — How We Check

What does "On-site" cover?
Everything on your own website — page titles, descriptions, headings, images, speed, and code quality. These are things you or your developer can fix directly.
What does "Indexing" mean?
Whether Google can find and read your pages. We check your sitemap, robots.txt, broken pages, redirects, and duplicate content.
What is "Linking & Social"?
Other websites linking to yours and your social media presence. This takes time and outreach to improve.
Where does backlink data come from?
We use Common Crawl, a free public index of the web. For a full backlink report, use Ahrefs or Semrush.
What is AI Readiness?
How well your content is structured for AI tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews — Schema markup, FAQ sections, and clear Q&A formats.
